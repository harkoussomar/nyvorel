A desktop should feel like one system.

I've been building Nyvorel, an open-source desktop experience for Arch Linux with Hyprland and Quickshell. It connects the bar, sidebars, settings, appearance tools, operations, and recovery in one visual and interaction system.

The most interesting work went beyond the interface: I built a backup-first installation and update path, made service ownership clearer, and moved selected on-demand utilities to Rust. It taught me how much of desktop design is really about making change and recovery understandable.

Nyvorel began as a derivative of end-4/dots-hyprland. I've redesigned and extended it substantially, and I want to credit that foundation.

Explore: https://nyvorel-web.vercel.app/
